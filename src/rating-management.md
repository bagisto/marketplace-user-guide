# Rating Management

Customers can rate and review both the **products** they buy and the **sellers** they buy from. Ratings build trust across the marketplace — sellers see their feedback, and the admin moderates what appears on the storefront.

::: info What you'll learn
- The two kinds of review
- Where a seller sees their ratings
- How the admin moderates reviews
:::

## Seller reviews

A customer can review a seller's service and leave a star rating. The seller sees these under **Performance → Seller Reviews**, with the customer, the review's **status** and date, and the **title, rating, and description**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/rating-management/seller-reviews.png" alt="Seller reviews list in the Seller Panel" />

## Product reviews

Customers also review individual products. A seller finds reviews of their listings under **Performance → Product Reviews**. These are the familiar storefront star ratings, scoped to the seller's own products.

## Moderation

Reviews aren't published automatically. Each one carries a **status** and is moderated by the admin before it goes live:

- **Seller reviews** — moderated under **Marketplace → Sellers → Seller Reviews** in the admin panel.
- **Product reviews** — moderated under **Marketplace → Catalog → Product Reviews**.

The admin approves or disapproves each review, so only vetted feedback reaches the storefront.

::: tip
Encourage buyers to leave reviews — strong ratings feed into [Featured Sellers](/featured-sellers) and help your [account health](/seller-account-health).
:::
