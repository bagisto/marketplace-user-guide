# Seller Quick Start

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--seller">Seller</span></div>

This page follows a new seller from sign-up to their first payout. Each step links
to the page with the details.

## 1. Create your seller account

Open the marketplace landing page from the store icon in the storefront header,
and fill in the **Become Seller** form.
See [Becoming a Seller](../sellers/becoming-a-seller.md).

## 2. Wait for approval and sign in

If the store approves new sellers, you can sign in at **Seller Login** once your
account is approved. You'll get an email when that happens.

## 3. Complete your profile

Add your phone number, business name and description, and your full address:
address, city, state, postcode and country. You can't list products or use bulk
upload until these are filled in. A logo, banner and policies are optional, but they
help customers trust your shop.
See [Manage Profile](../seller-panel/manage-profile.md).

## 4. Choose a plan

If the store uses subscription plans, choose one before you start selling.
See [Choosing a Plan](../subscriptions/choosing-a-plan.md).

## 5. Add your products

- Create your own products. See [Product Management](../catalog/product-management.md).
- Sell products already in the store's catalog at your own price. See [Selling Existing Products](../catalog/selling-existing-products.md).
- Add many at once from a spreadsheet. See [Bulk Upload](../catalog/bulk-upload.md).
- Let Generative AI write the listing. See [Generate Product Content](../generative-ai/generate-product-content.md).

## 6. Set stock and prices

Keep quantities and prices up to date across all your products on one screen.
See [Manage Inventory](../inventory/manage-inventory.md) and [Manage Pricing](../pricing/manage-pricing.md).

## 7. Submit for approval

Submit each product when it is ready. If the store approves products, it goes on
sale once approved.
See [Product Approval](../catalog/product-approval.md).

## 8. Promote your shop

Run discounts on your products, create checkout deals and coupons, and join the
store's own discounts.
See [Product Discounts](../marketing/product-discounts.md) and [Checkout Deals](../marketing/checkout-deals.md).

## 9. Fulfil your orders

Invoice each order, schedule the pickup, and handle returns.
See [Order Management](../orders/order-management.md) and [Shipments & Carriers](../orders/fulfilment-carriers.md).

## 10. Get paid

When an order is complete, request payment for it. The store pays you what you
earned, less its commission.
See [Payment Requests & Payouts](../payments/payment-management.md) and [Transactions](../payments/transactions.md).

## 11. Track how you are doing

Follow sales on your dashboard, keep your account health good, read reviews and
check your reports.
See [Dashboard](../seller-panel/dashboard.md), [Account Health](../sellers/seller-account-health.md)
and [Seller Reports](../reporting/seller-reporting.md).
