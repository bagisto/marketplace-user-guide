# Admin Quick Start

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--admin">Admin</span></div>

This page walks you through setting up the marketplace and the work that follows,
in the order you'll usually need it. Each step links to the page with the details.

## Set up the marketplace

### 1. Turn it on and set your commission

Make sure the marketplace is on, and choose the commission your store keeps from
each seller sale.
See [General](../configuration/general.md) and [Commission](../payments/commission-management.md).

### 2. Decide how sellers join

Choose whether new sellers wait for your approval, whether their shops are public,
and what they can do with orders.
See [Sellers settings](../configuration/sellers.md).

### 3. Decide how products are added

Choose whether sellers create their own products, sell products from your catalog,
or both, and whether you approve each product first.
See [Products settings](../configuration/products.md).

### 4. Set up the landing page

Write the page that invites visitors to become sellers, and choose which sellers to
feature on it.
See [Landing Page](../configuration/landing-page.md) and
[Featured Sellers](../configuration/featured-sellers.md).

### 5. Add what your marketplace needs

- **Subscription plans** to charge sellers. See [Plans & Subscriptions](../subscriptions/seller-subscription-management.md).
- **Generative AI** for seller listings. See [Generative AI Configuration](../configuration/generative-ai.md).
- **Reporting** of products and sellers, and account-health thresholds. See [Moderation & Health](../configuration/moderation-and-health.md).
- **Extra seller fields**, such as a tax number. See [Seller Attributes](../sellers/seller-attributes.md).

## Run the marketplace

### Approve sellers

Review new sign-ups and approve or disapprove them.
See [Seller Management](../sellers/seller-management.md).

### Approve products

Approve or reject the products sellers submit.
See [Product Approval](../catalog/product-approval.md).

### Follow orders and returns

See every seller's part of each order, and handle returns.
See [Order Management](../orders/order-management.md) and [Returns (RMA)](../orders/return-merchandise-authorization.md).

### Pay sellers

Pay sellers for completed orders when they request it, one at a time or several at
once.
See [Payment Requests & Payouts](../payments/payment-management.md).

### Keep the marketplace healthy

- Watch each seller's cancellations, ratings and flags in [Account Health](../sellers/seller-account-health.md).
- Approve reviews in [Reviews & Ratings](../moderation/rating-management.md).
- Look into reports in [Seller & Product Flagging](../moderation/seller-product-flagging.md).
- Answer sellers in [Communications](../customers/communications.md).
