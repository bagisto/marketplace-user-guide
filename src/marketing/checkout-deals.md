# Checkout Deals

Checkout Deals are cart-level promotions — discounts and coupons that apply to the **shopping cart** rather than to a single product. Use them to reward a whole order (for example, once it passes a spend threshold), optionally behind a coupon code the customer enters at checkout.

::: info What you'll learn
- How a checkout deal differs from a product discount
- How to create a checkout deal
- How to gate a deal behind a coupon
:::

## Creating a checkout deal

From the Seller Panel, open **Marketing → Checkout Deals** and select **Create Checkout Deal**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/checkout-deals/create.png" alt="Create Checkout Deal form" />

A checkout deal is built from two halves:

- **Conditions** — what the cart must satisfy for the deal to apply (for example, a minimum cart subtotal, or a certain quantity of items). Leave the conditions open to apply the deal to every qualifying cart.
- **Reward** — what the deal grants when the conditions are met (for example, a percentage or fixed discount on the cart total, or free shipping).

Set the deal's active dates and status, then save it. From then on, it applies automatically to qualifying carts at checkout.

## Adding a coupon

A deal can apply automatically, or only when a customer enters a **coupon code**. Enable and configure the coupon for the deal, and the reward is granted only to carts that enter the code — handy for targeted campaigns, newsletters, or influencer codes.

## Copying a deal

To build a similar promotion quickly, use **Copy** on an existing deal. It duplicates the deal's conditions and reward into a new draft you can adjust and save — faster than rebuilding from scratch for a recurring campaign.

::: tip
Reach for a Checkout Deal when the offer depends on the **whole cart** (spend $X, get Y) or needs a **coupon code**. To simply lower the shown price of individual products, use [Product Discounts](/marketing/product-discounts) or the [Manage Pricing](/pricing/manage-pricing) special price instead.
:::
