# Seller Account Health

**Account Health** is a single score that tells a seller how they're performing against the marketplace's standards — driven by cancellations, customer ratings, and flags. The admin uses the same score to spot sellers who need attention.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where a seller sees their health score
- What each metric measures and its target
- How the score is refreshed
:::

## The seller's view

In the **Seller Panel**, go to **Performance → Account Health**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-account-health/account-health.png" alt="Seller Account Health page" />

At the top, **Overall Health** summarises the seller's standing (*Healthy*, *At Risk*, or *Unhealthy*) with a **Health Score** out of 100 on a colour-coded bar. Below it, three cards break the score down:

- **Order Cancellation Rate** — the share of ordered units the seller cancelled over the last 30 days. *Target: below 2.5%.*
- **Customer Feedback** — the seller's average rating across all approved reviews. *Target: 4.0 and above.*
- **Flags** — the number of flags customers raised against the store in the last 90 days, against a red-flag limit.

**How is this calculated?** opens a plain-language explanation of the scoring.

## Refreshing the score

Health is recalculated automatically every day. A seller can also select **Refresh now** to recalculate on demand — useful right after resolving cancellations or clearing flags.

::: info For the admin
The admin sees each seller's health in the **Sellers** list and can refresh it per seller or in bulk. The thresholds are configured under **Configure → Marketplace → Moderation**. See [Seller Management](/sellers/seller-management) and [Seller & Product Flagging](/moderation/seller-product-flagging).
:::
