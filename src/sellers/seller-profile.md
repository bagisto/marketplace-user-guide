# Manage Profile

Manage Profile is the seller's own record of who they are — the shop name, description, policies, address and social links that customers see, plus the logo and banner that brand the store page. It's also where a seller checks what the admin has granted them.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where to find the profile screen
- What you can change, and what is read-only
- Why keeping the profile complete matters
:::

## Finding it

Open **Manage Profile** from your account menu in the Seller Panel header, or follow the **Complete Your Profile →** link on the [Dashboard](/sellers/seller-dashboard).

A **channel** and **locale** switcher at the top lets you keep different values per storefront and per language, so a shop selling into two channels can describe itself differently in each.

## Logo and banner

Two images brand your storefront:

**Logo:** Upload in a ratio of **1:1**.
**Banner:** Upload in a ratio of **3:1**. A resolution of **1320 × 300** is recommended.

Each has an **Upload New** control and a **Delete** control, so you can replace or clear either one. How large a file you can upload is capped by the admin's **Max File Upload Size** setting.

## Your details

The rest of the form is built from the [seller attributes](/sellers/seller-attributes) the admin has marked as visible on the profile, arranged into the groups and columns set on the attribute **Mapping** screen. On a default marketplace that means business details, a shop description, return / shipping / privacy policies, meta description, address, and social links.

Fill in what applies and select **Save Profile**.

## Shop Information

A read-only panel beside the form shows what the admin has decided for your shop. You can't change any of it here — it's shown so you know the terms you're selling under:

- **Admin Commission** — the rate applied to your sales, expressed as a percentage of the total invoiced amount. See [Commission Management](/payments/commission-management).
- **Allowed Product Type** — the kinds of product you may list.
- **Restricted Categories** — categories you may not sell into.
- **Payment Methods** and **Shipping Methods** — what is available on your orders.
- **Magic AI** — whether it's enabled for your shop, and if so your **Generation Limit**, **Limit Period** (**Daily** or **Monthly**), and how much you have **Used In Current Period**. An unlimited quota shows as **Unlimited**. See [Magic AI](/catalog/magic-ai).

Where a section has nothing in it, the panel says so rather than showing an empty list — for example, no categories or no payment methods available.

::: warning A complete profile unlocks listing
The product create flow is only available once your profile is complete. If it isn't, the **List Your Product** launcher sends you here first. See [Product Management](/catalog/product-management#adding-a-product).
:::

::: tip
Your profile is what a shopper reads on your [store page](/storefront/seller-storefront) before deciding to buy — the description, the policies and the banner do the selling when you aren't there to do it.
:::
