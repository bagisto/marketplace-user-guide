# Featured Sellers

Featured Sellers spotlights a set of sellers on the storefront — an **Our Featured Sellers** showcase — to give strong or promising sellers extra visibility. The admin turns it on and chooses how sellers are selected; the marketplace then fills the showcase automatically.

::: info What you'll learn
- What Featured Sellers does
- How the admin enables and tunes it
- How sellers are chosen
:::

## Enabling and tuning

In the admin panel, go to **Configure → Featured Sellers**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/featured-sellers/featured-sellers-config.png" alt="Featured Sellers configuration" />

- **Status** — turn the featured showcase on or off.
- **Limit Count** — how many sellers to feature (for example, **12**).
- **Popularity Criteria** — how featured sellers are ranked and picked:
  - **All** — rank by orders, products, rating, and sales together.
  - **Maximum Orders** — the sellers with the most orders.
  - **Maximum Products** — the sellers with the largest catalogs.
  - **Maximum Rating** — the highest-rated sellers (by approved reviews).
  - **Maximum Sale** — the sellers with the highest sales value.

Save the configuration, and the storefront shows the featured sellers accordingly.

## How the selection works

Featured placement is **earned, not assigned**. The marketplace ranks eligible sellers by your chosen criteria and takes the top **Limit Count**. Only sellers who are **approved**, **not suspended**, have a business name, and belong to the current channel are eligible — so the showcase always reflects real, active sellers.

::: tip
Because placement rewards performance, the way for a seller to get featured is simply to sell well and earn good reviews — which ties straight back to [Rating Management](/moderation/rating-management) and [Seller Account Health](/sellers/seller-account-health).
:::
