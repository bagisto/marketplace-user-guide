# Seller Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen a seller sees after signing in — a single read on how the store is doing right now, what needs attention today, and how complete the seller's own profile is.

::: info What you'll learn
- What each block of the dashboard shows
- How the date range changes the figures
- What the "Things to do" list is telling you
:::

## The greeting and profile score

The page opens with **Hi! *your name*** and a short prompt to review what's going on in your store. Beside it sits your **Profile Score** — how complete your seller profile is — with a **Complete Your Profile →** link straight to [Manage Profile](/sellers/seller-profile).

The score is worth clearing early: an incomplete profile blocks the product create flow, so a low score is usually the reason a seller can't list anything yet.

## The date range

A date-range control at the top governs every figure below it. Pick one of the presets — **Today**, **This Month**, **Last Month**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, **Last 6 Months**, **Last Year** — or set your own **Start Date** and **End Date**.

Changing the range re-reads the whole page, so the trends and totals always describe the same window.

## Overview

Three cards summarise the health of the store:

- **Account Health** — your current verdict: **Healthy**, **At Risk**, **Unhealthy**, or **Not Calculated** if there isn't enough data yet. See [Seller Account Health](/sellers/seller-account-health).
- **Buyer Messages** — how many conversations are waiting, with an **Unread** count. See [Communications](/customers/communications).
- **Customer Feedback** — your average rating and the number of reviews behind it. See [Rating Management](/moderation/rating-management).

Below them, **Trends** charts **Sales** and **Orders** across the selected range.

### Things to do

A short worklist of everything currently waiting on you:

- **Orders awaiting fulfilment**
- **Products awaiting approval**
- **Products low on stock**
- **Unread buyer messages**

When there is nothing outstanding it reads **You are all caught up!** — so an empty list is the goal, not a sign the block is broken.

## Overall Details

Six figures for the selected range:

**Total Sales**, **Total Payout**, **Remaining Payout**, **Total Orders**, **Total Customers**, and **Average Order Sell**.

**Total Payout** is what has already been settled with you; **Remaining Payout** is what you're still owed. Both come from the same ledger the admin pays you from — see [Payment Management](/payments/payment-management).

## Stock Threshold

The products running low on stock, each with its remaining quantity, and a **View All** link into [Manage Inventory](/inventory/manage-inventory). What counts as "low" is the **Low Stock Threshold** the admin sets under **Configure → Marketplace → Products → Inventory**.

When nothing is low it shows **Empty Threshold** — there is no product available — which means every listing is comfortably in stock.

## Top Categories

Which of your categories sold best over the selected range. If nothing sold in that window it reads **No Data Available**, with a note that no category was found for the selected interval — widen the range to see more.

::: info Team members see the shop's figures
When a staff user signs in, the greeting and profile prompt are theirs, but every shop-level figure — health, orders, products, reviews — belongs to the shop as a whole. A team member sees the store's numbers, not an empty dashboard of their own. See [Seller Users & Roles](/sellers/seller-user-role-management).
:::

::: tip
Work the **Things to do** list first and the rest of the dashboard tends to look after itself — unshipped orders and low stock are the two things that turn into cancellations, and cancellations are the fastest way to damage [account health](/sellers/seller-account-health).
:::
