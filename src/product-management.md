# Product Management

Product Management is where a seller builds and maintains their catalog — creating listings, editing details, and tracking each product's approval status. Everything a seller lists goes through the admin's approval before it reaches the storefront.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where a seller's products live and what the list shows
- How to create a new product with the guided flow
- How the product form is organised, and how listings get approved
:::

## The products list

From the Seller Panel, open **Catalog → Products**. Every product the seller owns appears here, with its image, price, stock, ID, **status** (Active / Draft, and the approval state such as Approved), type, and barcode state.

<ImagePopup src="/images/product-management/products-list.png" alt="Seller products list" />

Use **Search** and **Filter** to narrow the list, the row menu to edit or delete, and the checkboxes for bulk actions.

## Creating a product

Product creation starts from the **List Your Product** launcher in the sidebar. It offers three ways to add products:

- **Create a new product** — build a brand-new listing from scratch with a short guided flow.
- **Search product** — list something already in the marketplace catalog as your own. See [Selling Existing Products](/selling-existing-products).
- **Bulk upload** — add many products at once from a spreadsheet. See [Bulk Upload](/bulk-upload).

Choosing **Create a new product** opens a plain-language funnel that maps your answers to the right product type behind the scenes:

- **What kind of product are you selling?** — Standard, Digital Download, Service or Membership, Bookable, Bundle, or Grouped.
- **Does this product have variations?** — answer **Yes** to create a product with variants (for example size or colour), or **No** for a single item.
- **Which attribute family** the product belongs to, and the **SKU** to use.

::: info Complete your profile first
The create flow is only available once your seller profile is complete. If it isn't, the launcher prompts you to finish your profile details before you can list a product.
:::

## The product form

After the funnel, the product opens on the edit form. A **Listing Completeness** bar at the top shows how much of the listing is filled in — a more complete listing gets more visibility. The form is organised into two phases (**Product Basics** → **Complete Listing**) with tabbed sections:

<ImagePopup src="/images/product-management/product-edit.png" alt="Seller product edit form" />

- **General** — SKU, product number, name, and URL key.
- **Price** — selling price, cost, and special (sale) price with a date range. See [Manage Pricing](/manage-pricing) for bulk pricing.
- **Description** — short and full descriptions. Sellers with access can draft these with [Magic AI](/magic-ai).
- **Shipping** — width, height, length, and weight for shipping calculations.
- **Meta Description** — meta title, keywords, and description for SEO.
- **Settings** — toggles such as New, Featured, Visible Individually, Status, and Guest Checkout.
- **Inventories** — stock per inventory source. See [Manage Inventory](/manage-inventory).
- **RMA** — the product's return policy (whether returns are allowed and under what terms).
- **Barcode** — the product's barcode value and auto-generated image. See [Product Barcode](/product-barcode).
- **Media** — product images and video.
- **Organization** — attribute family and the categories the product belongs to.

You can **Submit** the product for approval, or **Save as Draft** to keep working on it privately — see [Draft Products](/draft-products).

## Approval

A submitted product isn't live immediately. It stays in a **pending / disapproved** state until the admin reviews it under **Marketplace → Products** in the admin panel. Once the admin approves it, the status changes to **Approved** and the product appears on the storefront. If the admin rejects it, they can attach a reason the seller sees on the listing.

::: tip
Keep the **Listing Completeness** bar as close to 100% as you can before submitting — complete listings are easier to approve and rank better on the storefront.
:::
