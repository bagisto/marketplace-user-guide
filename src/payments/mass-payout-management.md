# Mass Payout Management

When a marketplace has many sellers with pending earnings, paying them one at a time is slow. **Mass payout** lets the admin settle multiple payable orders — across one or many sellers — in a single action, from the same Seller Payout Management screen.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where mass payout lives
- How to pay several orders at once
- What the summary tells you before you pay
:::

## The payout view

Open **Marketplace → Sales → Payment Requests** (**Seller Payout Management**). The summary cards give you the outstanding picture — **Payable Orders**, **Unpaid Payout Amount**, **Total Commission Earned**, and **Unpaid Sellers** — before you pay anything, and the filters let you narrow the list by seller, customer, amount, status, or date.

<ImagePopup src="/images/mass-payout-management/payout-management.png" alt="Seller Payout Management with payable orders" />

## Paying in bulk

Select the payable orders you want to settle with the row checkboxes, then use the bulk **Pay Now** action to pay them together. Each order is settled for its full remaining amount and stamped with a system comment, exactly as an individual [payment](/payments/payment-management) is — just processed as a batch — and each records a [transaction](/payments/payment-management#transactions).

::: tip
Use the summary cards and filters to target a payout run — for example, clear every fully-invoiced order for the month in one pass, rather than chasing sellers individually.
:::
