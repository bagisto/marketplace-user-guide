# Account Settings

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--seller">Seller</span></div>

Account settings belong to the person signed in, whether that is the shop owner or
a team member. Use them to change your password and the language of the Seller
Panel. The shop owner can also choose whether to get emails about customer messages.

Go to **Settings >> General**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-panel/account-settings.png" alt="The General settings page in the Seller Panel" />

## Change your password

1. Go to **Settings >> General**.
2. In **Password Settings**, enter your **Current Password**.
3. Enter a **New Password** of at least 6 characters, and enter it again in
   **Confirm Password**.
4. Click **Save**.

Leave these fields empty to keep your current password.

## Choose the Seller Panel language

1. Go to **Settings >> General**.
2. In **Regional Settings**, choose a **Locale**.
3. Click **Save**.

**Currency** shows the store's currency. It can't be changed here.

## Emails about customer messages

In **Communication Settings**, switch **Receive email for customer messages** off if
you don't want an email each time a customer writes to you. Only the shop owner gets
these emails. You still see the messages under **Communication >> Customers**. Click
**Save** to keep the change.

When you save, you'll see **General settings updated successfully**. If you are the
shop owner, you also receive a profile update email.
