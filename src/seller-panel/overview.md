# Seller Panel Overview

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--seller">Seller</span></div>

The Seller Panel is where sellers and their team run their shop. It opens at
`/seller` on your store's address, and you sign in on **Seller Login**. See
[Becoming a Seller](../sellers/becoming-a-seller.md).

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-panel/seller-panel.png" alt="The Seller Panel with its header, sidebar and dashboard" />

## The header

- **Mega Search** — type to find your **Products**, **Orders** and **Customers**.
  Click a product or order to open it, or use **Explore all** to see every match.
  Customer results can't be clicked, so use **Explore all** to find them in your
  customers list.
- **Toggle Theme** — switch between the light and dark look. Your choice is kept in
  your browser.
- **Home Page** — opens the store's home page.
- **Visit Shop** — opens your shop page as customers see it. It appears when the
  store makes seller shops public.
- **Profile menu** — **My Profile** opens your shop details, and **Logout** signs
  you out.

When the store uses subscription plans, your plan ends within nine days and no next
plan is lined up, the header also shows **Subscription expires in:** with a countdown. Click it to choose
your next plan.

## The sidebar

| Menu | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Follow sales, orders and what needs your attention. See [Dashboard](dashboard.md). |
| **Catalog** | List products, see your products and bulk upload. See [Product Management](../catalog/product-management.md). |
| **Inventories** | Update stock and manage your inventory sources. See [Manage Inventory](../inventory/manage-inventory.md). |
| **Pricing** | Update prices, sale prices and tier prices. See [Manage Pricing](../pricing/manage-pricing.md). |
| **Marketing** | Run product discounts and checkout deals. See [Product Discounts](../marketing/product-discounts.md). |
| **Sales** | Handle orders, returns, bookings and your transactions. See [Order Management](../orders/order-management.md). |
| **Performance** | Check account health and read reviews. See [Account Health](../sellers/seller-account-health.md). |
| **Reporting** | See sales, customer and product reports. See [Seller Reports](../reporting/seller-reporting.md). |
| **Communication** | Message the store admin and reply to customers. See [Communications](../customers/communications.md). |
| **Customers** | See the customers who bought from you. See [Seller Customers](../customers/customer-management.md). |
| **Plans** | Choose a subscription plan. It appears for the shop owner when the store uses plans. See [Choosing a Plan](../subscriptions/choosing-a-plan.md). |
| **Settings** | Change your password and language, and manage your team. See [Account Settings](account-settings.md) and [Users & Roles](users-and-roles.md). |

Click **Collapse** at the bottom of the sidebar to show icons only.

The pages inside a menu also appear as tabs at the top of the page, so you can move
between them without opening the sidebar.

## What team members see

Team members sign in on the same **Seller Login** page. They see only the menus
their role allows, and they work with the shop's data. They can't open
**My Profile**, which is only for the shop owner. See [Users & Roles](users-and-roles.md).

## Change the panel language

The Seller Panel uses the language chosen under **Settings >> General**. See
[Account Settings](account-settings.md).
