# Dashboard

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--seller">Seller</span></div>

The dashboard is usually the first page you see after you sign in. When the store
uses subscription plans, you land on **My Profile** instead. The dashboard shows how
your shop is doing and what needs your attention.

Go to **Dashboard**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-panel/dashboard.png" alt="The Seller Panel dashboard" />

## Choose the period

Use the date picker at the top right to change the period the figures cover. Pick
a preset, such as **Today**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, **This Month** or
**This Year**, or choose your own start and end dates.

## What the dashboard shows

### Profile Score

How complete your shop details are, as a percentage. While your score is below
100%, click **Complete Your Profile** to fill in what's missing. Only the shop owner sees this
card. See [Manage Profile](manage-profile.md).

### Account Health, Buyer Messages and Customer Feedback

- **Account Health** — your current health, such as **Healthy** or **At Risk**. See
  [Account Health](../sellers/seller-account-health.md).
- **Buyer Messages** — how many messages you haven't read. Click it to open your
  customer conversations.
- **Customer Feedback** — your shop's average rating and number of approved reviews.

### Trends

Small charts of your **Sales** and **Orders** over the period.

### Things to do

A list of what is waiting for you, with a link to each:

- **Orders awaiting fulfilment** — orders that are pending or processing.
- **Products awaiting approval** — products you submitted that the admin hasn't
  approved yet.
- **Products low on stock** — products at or below the store's low-stock level.
- **Unread buyer messages** — messages from customers and the admin.

When nothing is waiting, it shows **You are all caught up!**

### Overall Details

**Total Sales**, **Total Payout**, **Remaining Payout**, **Total Orders**,
**Total Customers** and **Average Order Sell** for the period.

### Stock Threshold

Up to five of your products that are at or below the store's low-stock level. Click
a product to edit it, or update stock for many products in **Manage Inventory**.

### Top Categories

The categories that sold the most in the period.

## What team members see

Team members with the **Dashboard** permission see the same figures for the shop,
without the **Profile Score** card.
