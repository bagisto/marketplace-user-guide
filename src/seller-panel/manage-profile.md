# Manage Profile

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--seller">Seller</span></div>

Your profile holds your shop's details: the name and logo customers see, your
description and policies, your address and your contact details. Customers see much
of it on your shop page.

Only the shop owner can open this page. To open it, click your profile icon at the
top right of the Seller Panel, then click **My Profile**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-panel/manage-profile.png" alt="The Manage Profile page" />

## Complete your profile

You can't list products until the required details are filled in.

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Upload your banner and logo. Use the menu on each image and choose
   **Upload New Banner** or **Upload New Logo**. A banner in a 3:1 ratio, around
   1320×300 pixels, and a square logo fit best. Each image must be within the
   store's upload limit, 10 MB unless the store changes it.
3. In **General**, check your **Name**, **Email**, **Slug** and **Business Name**,
   and enter your **Phone**.
4. In **Description**, write your **Business Description**.
5. In **Address**, enter your **Address**, **City**, **Country**, **State** and
   **Postcode**.
6. Fill in the optional cards, such as **Policies** and **Social Links**.
7. Click **Save Profile**.

You'll see **Your Profile is updated successfully** and receive a confirmation
email.

If your store has more than one channel or language, choose it at the top of the
page before you edit the fields marked with a channel or language name.

## The profile cards

The store decides which cards and fields appear. By default they are:

| Card | Fields |
|---|---|
| **General** | Name, Email, Phone, Slug, Business Name |
| **Description** | Business Description |
| **Policies** | Return Policy, Shipping Policy, Privacy Policy |
| **Meta Description** | Meta Title, Meta Keywords, Meta Description, with a preview of how your shop appears in search results |
| **Address** | Address, City, Country, State, Postcode |
| **Social Links** | Facebook, Instagram, YouTube |
| **Settings** | Minimum Order Amount, Google Analytics ID |

Changing your **Slug** changes the address of your shop page, so links you have
shared before stop working.

**Minimum Order Amount** is the least a customer must spend with you in one order.
It appears when the store allows it.

## Shop Information

The **Shop Information** card shows what the store has set for you. When the store
uses subscription plans, it appears only while you have an active plan. You can't
change it here:

- **Admin Commission** — the commission the store takes from your invoiced sales.
- **Magic AI** — whether Generative AI is on for your shop, your limit, and how many
  generations you have used. It appears when the store uses Generative AI. See
  [Usage Limits](../generative-ai/usage-limits.md).
- **Restricted Categories** — categories you can't use.
- **Allowed Product Type** — the product types you can sell.
- **Payment Methods** and **Shipping Methods** — what the store offers customers at
  checkout.

## Where customers see your profile

Your banner, logo, business name and address appear at the top of your shop page.
Your description and the policies you filled in appear in its **About** tab. See
[Seller Shop Page](../storefront/seller-shop-page.md).
