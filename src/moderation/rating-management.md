# Rating Management

Customers can rate and review both the **products** they buy and the **sellers** they buy from. Ratings build trust across the marketplace — sellers see their feedback, and the admin moderates everything before it appears on the storefront.

::: info What you'll learn
- The two kinds of review and who can leave them
- Where a seller sees their ratings
- How the admin moderates reviews
:::

## Who can review

A review can only be left by a customer who has actually **bought** the product (or bought from the seller), and each customer can review a given product or seller **once**. Every review starts as **pending** and stays hidden until an admin approves it — so the storefront only ever shows vetted feedback.

## The seller's view

- **Seller reviews** — a customer can rate a seller's service and shop. The seller sees these under **Performance → Seller Reviews**, with the customer, **rating**, **title**, **description**, **status**, and **date**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/rating-management/seller-reviews.png" alt="Seller reviews in the Seller Panel" />

- **Product reviews** — customers also review individual products; the seller finds reviews of their listings under **Performance → Product Reviews**. Both views are read-only — moderation is the admin's job.

## Moderation (admin)

Reviews carry a **status** — **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Disapproved** — and the admin decides which go live:

- **Seller reviews** — under **Marketplace → Sellers → Seller Reviews**, the admin can **approve**, **disapprove**, or **delete** a review, individually or in bulk.

<ImagePopup src="/images/rating-management/admin-seller-reviews.png" alt="Admin seller review moderation" />

- **Product reviews** — under **Marketplace → Catalog → Product Reviews**, the admin can **view** a review and **approve / disapprove** it, individually or in bulk.

<ImagePopup src="/images/rating-management/admin-product-reviews.png" alt="Admin product review moderation" />

Only **approved** reviews appear on the storefront.

::: tip
Encourage buyers to leave reviews — a strong, approved rating history feeds into [Featured Sellers](/sellers/featured-sellers) and lifts your [account health](/sellers/seller-account-health).
:::
