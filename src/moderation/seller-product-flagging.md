# Seller & Product Flagging

Flagging lets customers report a problem with a **product** or a **seller** — a wrong, damaged, duplicate, or overpriced listing, or an issue with the seller. The admin defines the reasons customers can pick and reviews the flags that come in.

::: info What you'll learn
- What customers can flag and why
- How the admin defines flag reasons
- Where flags are reviewed
:::

## Flag reasons

The admin controls the list of reasons a customer may choose when flagging. Go to **Marketplace → Configure → Flag Reasons**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-product-flagging/flag-reasons.png" alt="Flag reasons list" />

Each reason has a **type** — **Product** or **Seller** — and a **status**:

- **Product** reasons apply to a listing, e.g. *Receive wrong product*, *Missing product parts*, *Damaged product*, *Poor product quality*.
- **Seller** reasons apply to the seller, e.g. *Wrong product sold by seller*, *Over price product sold by seller*, *Duplicate product sold by seller*.

Use **Create Reason** to add one, and enable or disable reasons to control what customers can pick.

## Reviewing flags

When customers flag products or sellers, the admin reviews them from the admin panel — flags raised against a product from **Marketplace → Catalog → Products**, and flags raised against a seller from **Marketplace → Sellers**. This gives the admin the signal they need to investigate a listing or seller and act.

::: tip
Keep your reasons specific and few — a short, clear list gets more useful reports than a long, overlapping one.
:::
