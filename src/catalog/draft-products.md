# Draft Products

A seller doesn't have to finish a product in one sitting. **Save as Draft** keeps a work-in-progress listing private — it isn't sent to the admin for approval and never appears on the storefront — until the seller is ready to submit it.

::: info What you'll learn
- How a product becomes (and stays) a draft
- How drafts appear in the products list
- How a draft is submitted for approval
:::

## How drafts happen

Every product you create from scratch starts life as a **draft**, so you can build it up safely. While creating or editing a product — or listing one with [Sell as Yours](/catalog/selling-existing-products) — the form gives you two choices:

<ImagePopup src="/images/draft-products/edit-draft-buttons.png" alt="Save as Draft and Submit on the product form" />

- **Save as Draft** — stores everything entered so far and keeps the product a draft. It skips validation, so you can save an incomplete listing and come back to it.
- **Submit** — validates the listing and sends it into the approval flow.

## Finding your drafts

In **Catalog → Products**, the **Draft Status** column marks each product as **Draft** or **Submitted**, and you can filter the list by it. A draft's price, stock, images, and every other detail are all saved and fully editable.

<ImagePopup src="/images/draft-products/products-list-draft.png" alt="Products list showing a draft" />

## Publishing a draft

Open the draft, complete the remaining details, and **Submit**. It then enters the normal approval flow — the admin reviews it, and once approved it goes live. See [Product Management](/catalog/product-management#drafts-submission-and-approval) for the approval side.

::: tip Rejection reasons
If the admin rejects a submitted product, they can attach a **reason**, which you'll see on the listing (its status shows **Rejected**) — so it's clear what to fix before resubmitting.
:::
