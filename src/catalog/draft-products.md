# Draft Products

A seller doesn't have to finish and submit a product in one sitting. **Save as Draft** keeps a work-in-progress listing private — it isn't sent to the admin for approval and never appears on the storefront — until the seller is ready.

::: info What you'll learn
- How a seller saves a product as a draft
- How drafts appear in the product list
- How a draft becomes a live listing
:::

## Saving a draft

While creating or editing a product — or listing an existing one with [Sell as Yours](/catalog/selling-existing-products) — select **Save as Draft** instead of **Submit**. Everything entered so far is stored, but the listing stays out of the approval queue.

## Finding your drafts

In **Catalog → Products**, a draft carries a **Draft** badge next to its status. Its price, stock, and images are all saved and editable.

<ImagePopup src="/images/draft-products/products-grid.png" alt="Seller products list showing a draft" />

## Publishing a draft

Open the draft, complete the remaining details, and **Submit** it. It then enters the normal approval flow — the admin reviews it, and once approved it goes live. See [Seller Management](/sellers/seller-management) for the approval side.

::: tip Rejection reasons
If the admin rejects a submitted product, they can attach a **reason**, which the seller sees on the listing — so it's clear what to fix before resubmitting.
:::
