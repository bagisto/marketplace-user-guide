# Selling Existing Products

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--seller">Seller</span></div>

When a product is already in the store's catalog, you don't need to create it again.
You can sell it as your own, with your own price, stock, condition and description.
The product's name, images and details stay as they are in the catalog.

Customers see your offer next to other sellers' offers for the same product. See
[Shopping Across Sellers](../storefront/shopping-across-sellers.md).

## Find the product

1. Go to **Catalog >> List Your Product**.
2. Click **Search Products**.
3. Type at least three letters of the product name.
4. Click **Sell as Yours** on the product you want.

<ImagePopup src="/images/selling-existing-products/search.png" alt="Search results with the Sell as Yours link" />

You can't sell a product when:

- the store doesn't allow sellers to sell catalog products (**You are not allowed to assign this product**);
- you already sell it (**You are already selling this product**);
- its product type isn't one you're allowed to sell.

## Create your offer

1. On **Assign Product**, choose the **Condition**: **New** or **Old**.
2. Enter your **Price**. For a product with variations, you set it for each variant
   instead.
3. Enter a **Description** of your offer, up to 300 characters.
4. Under **Inventories**, enter how many you have in each inventory source. This
   appears for simple and virtual products only.
5. Click **Submit**, or **Save as Draft** to finish later.

<ImagePopup src="/images/selling-existing-products/assign-form.png" alt="The Assign Product form for selling a catalog product" />

**Product Details** on the right shows the catalog product you are selling. The
**Condition**, **Price** and **Description** are needed when you submit, but you can
leave them empty in a draft.

You'll see **Product assigned successfully to the seller**. If the store approves
products, your offer waits for approval like any other product. See
[Product Approval](product-approval.md).

### Products with variations

For a product that comes in sizes or colours, you choose which variants to sell. For
each one, set its **Price**, **Quantities**, **Description** and **Images**. The
condition you choose applies to all of them.

### Downloadable products

For a downloadable product, add your own **Downloadable Links** and
**Downloadable Samples**.

## Update or remove your offer

- To change the price, stock or condition, open the product from
  **Catalog >> Products** and click **Save**.
- To stop selling it, delete it from **Catalog >> Products**. Only your offer is
  removed. The catalog product stays in the store.

To change prices or stock for many products at once, use
[Manage Pricing](../pricing/manage-pricing.md) and
[Manage Inventory](../inventory/manage-inventory.md).

## Good to know

- Your offer uses the catalog product's images. Only the variants of a product with
  variations can have their own images.
- To sell many catalog products at once, use **Sell Catalog Products** in
  [Bulk Upload](bulk-upload.md).
- If the store uses subscription plans, your offers count towards your plan's
  product limit.
- The store admin can also add a catalog product to your shop for you. See
  [Seller Management](../sellers/seller-management.md#assign-a-product-to-a-seller).
