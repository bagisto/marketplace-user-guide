# Customer Management

A seller can see the customers who have bought from them under **Customers** in the Seller Panel. It's a read-only view that helps a seller understand who their buyers are, without exposing the marketplace's full customer base.

::: info What you'll learn
- Which customers a seller can see
- What the customers view is for
:::

## Your customers

Open **Customers** in the Seller Panel. The list shows the customers who have placed orders for this seller's products, so a seller can recognise repeat buyers and understand their audience.

<ImagePopup src="/images/customer-management/customers.png" alt="Seller customers view" />

::: info Sellers see only their own buyers
A seller only sees customers who have bought from *them* — not every customer on the marketplace. Full customer management across the platform stays with the admin. For sales and buyer trends over time, see [Seller Reporting](/seller-reporting).
:::
