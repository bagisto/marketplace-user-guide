# Sellers

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--admin">Admin</span></div>

The **Sellers** screen decides whether new sellers need your approval, whether
their shops are public, and what they can do with their orders.

Go to **Configure >> Marketplace >> Sellers**.

## Registration & Visibility

<ImagePopup src="/images/configuration/sellers-registration.png" alt="Registration & Visibility section of the Sellers settings" />

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approval Required** | New sellers start as **Disapproved** and can't sign in until you approve them. This applies to sellers who sign up themselves and to sellers you add. When off, new sellers are approved straight away. |
| **Show Publicly** | Makes seller shop pages public. When off, shop pages can't be opened, the **Sold By** link is hidden on product pages, the featured sellers are hidden, and sellers lose the **Visit Shop** button in their panel. |
| **Can View Customers** | Lets sellers see customer email addresses and order locations. When off, these are masked in the seller's orders, customers, reviews and reports, and the customer's addresses, shipping method and payment method are hidden on the seller's order page. |

To approve sellers, see [Seller Management](../sellers/seller-management.md).

## Order Capabilities

<ImagePopup src="/images/configuration/sellers-order-capabilities.png" alt="Order Capabilities and Uploads sections" />

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Can Create Invoice** | Shows the **Invoice** button on the seller's order page. |
| **Can Create Shipment** | Shows **Schedule Pickup** on the seller's orders and order page. |
| **Can Cancel Order** | Shows the **Cancel** button on the seller's order page and lets sellers cancel their items. |
| **Enable Minimum Order Amount** | Lets each seller have a minimum order amount. The **Minimum Order Amount** field appears on the seller's profile. When a customer's items from that seller add up to less, the cart shows **Minimum order amount for … is: …** and the customer can't check out until they add more. |

A seller also needs the matching permission in their role to use each button.
See [Order Management](../orders/order-management.md) and
[Shopping Across Sellers](../storefront/shopping-across-sellers.md).

## Uploads

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Max File Upload Size** | The largest file, in MB, a seller can upload for profile fields and for the image ZIP in bulk upload. Leave it empty to allow 10 MB. |
