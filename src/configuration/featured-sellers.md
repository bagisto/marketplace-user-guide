# Featured Sellers

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--admin">Admin</span></div>

The **Featured Sellers** screen controls the block of popular sellers on the
marketplace landing page.

Go to **Configure >> Marketplace >> Featured Sellers**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configuration/featured-sellers.png" alt="Featured Sellers settings" />

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Shows the **Our Featured Sellers** block on the landing page. |
| **Limit Count** | How many sellers the block shows. |
| **Popularity Criteria** | How sellers are ranked: **Maximum Orders**, **Maximum Products**, **Maximum Rating** or **Maximum Sale**. **All** ranks by orders first, then products, rating and sales. |

All three are saved for each channel.

The block only appears while **Show Publicly** is on in
[Sellers](sellers.md). It lists only approved sellers who aren't suspended, sell on
the current channel and have a business name. When subscription plans are on, they
also need an active plan.

See [Featured Sellers](../storefront/featured-sellers.md) for what customers see.
