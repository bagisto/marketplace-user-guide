# Products

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--admin">Admin</span></div>

The **Products** screen decides how sellers add products, whether you approve
them first, and how the seller product form looks.

Go to **Configure >> Marketplace >> Products**.

## Approval & Creation

<ImagePopup src="/images/configuration/products-approval.png" alt="Approval & Creation section of the Products settings" />

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approval Required** | New seller products wait for your approval before they can be sold. This covers products sellers create, existing products they sell, and products they import. When off, they are approved as soon as the seller submits them. |
| **Seller Can Create Product** | Lets sellers create their own products, one at a time or with bulk upload. |
| **Seller Can Assign Product** | Lets sellers sell products that are already in your catalog, at their own price and stock. |

See [Product Approval](../catalog/product-approval.md) and
[Selling Existing Products](../catalog/selling-existing-products.md).

## Product Form

<ImagePopup src="/images/configuration/products-form-inventory.png" alt="Product Form and Inventory sections" />

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable Stepped Product Form** | Shows sellers the product form as guided steps instead of one long page. |
| **Stepped Form Navigation** | How sellers move between steps: **Horizontal Tabs** or **Vertical Rail**. |
| **Show Progress Bar** | Shows a **Listing Completeness** bar on the seller's product edit page. |

These are the defaults for every attribute family. To give one family a different
layout or step order, see [Product Form Layout](../catalog/product-form-layout.md).

## Inventory

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Low Stock Threshold** | A simple or virtual product that manages its stock counts as low on stock on the seller's dashboard when its total stock is at or below this number. It is 10 until this screen is first saved. Enter a number here; don't leave it empty. |

See [Dashboard](../seller-panel/dashboard.md).
