# Fulfilment Carriers

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--admin">Admin</span></div>

Fulfilment carriers are the couriers sellers use to ship an order after it is
placed. They are never offered to customers at checkout: customers still choose
from your store's shipping methods.

Go to **Configure >> Marketplace >> Fulfilment Carriers**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configuration/fulfilment-carriers.png" alt="Self Ship (Manual) section of the Fulfilment Carriers settings" />

## Self Ship (Manual)

Self Ship is always available and can't be switched off. The seller arranges
their own courier and types the carrier name and tracking number when they
schedule a pickup.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title Shown To Sellers** | The name sellers see for this carrier. Leave it empty to show **Manual (Own Arrangement)**. It is saved for each channel and language. |

## Courier integrations

A courier integration is a separate package. Once installed, it adds its own
section to this screen with the settings it needs, such as account keys. A courier
is offered to sellers only while it is switched on and every required setting is
filled in.

See [Shipments & Carriers](../orders/fulfilment-carriers.md) for how sellers
schedule a pickup.
