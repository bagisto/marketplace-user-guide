# Seller Subscriptions

Seller Subscriptions let the marketplace charge sellers a recurring fee to sell, using **plans** that can bundle their own commission rate and selling limits. It's an **optional** feature — off by default — that the admin turns on when they want a subscription-based model.

::: info What you'll learn
- How to enable subscriptions
- What a subscription plan defines
- How sellers subscribe
:::

## Enabling subscriptions

Subscriptions are disabled until the admin switches them on. Go to **Configure → Marketplace → General** and enable **Subscription Plans** (and set the **subscription terms** sellers agree to). Until this is on, the plans area isn't available.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-subscription-management/subscription-settings.png" alt="Marketplace settings with the subscription switch" />

## Plans

Once enabled, the admin manages plans under **Marketplace → Plans**. A plan defines what a seller gets for subscribing — typically its price and billing cycle, its **commission** terms, and limits such as how many products the seller may list. Plans can be created, edited, and enabled or disabled.

## Subscribing

With subscriptions on, sellers choose a plan and subscribe from their panel. The admin can review who's subscribed to what under the plan's **subscriptions** view, and a subscribed seller's [commission](/payments/commission-management) follows their plan.

::: info
If you run a commission-only marketplace, simply leave subscriptions off — sellers then sell without a recurring fee and you earn purely through commission.
:::
