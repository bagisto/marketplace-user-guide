# Choosing a Plan

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--seller">Seller</span></div>

When the store uses subscription plans, each seller needs an active plan to sell. A
plan sets how many products and team members you can have, the commission the store
takes, and which parts of the Seller Panel you can use.

::: warning Without an active plan
- You can only open **My Profile** and **Plans** in the Seller Panel.
- Your team members can't open anything in the Seller Panel.
- Your products aren't shown to customers.
:::

While the store uses plans, every seller lands on **My Profile** after signing in.

## See the available plans

Go to **Plans >> Available Plans**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/choosing-a-plan/available-plans.png" alt="The Plans page with the available plans" />

If you have a plan, the page starts with **Current Plan**. Each plan card shows:

- the plan's name, with a star when the store recommends it;
- its price and how many days it lasts;
- **Max Products**, **Max Staff Ids** and **Admin Commission**;
- under **Permissions**, every part of the Seller Panel. Parts the plan doesn't include
  are shown in red with a cross.

## Buy a plan

1. Go to **Plans >> Available Plans**.
2. Click a plan. Its details open, with the description, the product types you can
   sell and the full list of permissions.
3. Click **terms and conditions** to read the store's terms.
4. Tick **I have read and agree with terms and conditions.**
5. Click **Proceed**.
6. For a paid plan, choose a payment method and complete the payment.

<ImagePopup src="/images/choosing-a-plan/plan-details.png" alt="The details of a plan with the terms checkbox" />

What happens next depends on the plan and how you pay:

| Plan or payment | Result |
|---|---|
| A free plan | It is active straight away. The free plan is offered only until you have had a plan that became active. |
| PayPal | The plan is active once the payment goes through. |
| Another payment method, such as Money Transfer | The plan stays **Pending** until the store admin approves it. Cash On Delivery isn't offered for plans. |

When you already have a plan:

- Buying a plan while you're on the free plan ends the free plan straight away.
- Buying a plan cancels any plan of yours that is still **Pending**.
- Otherwise, the new plan starts when your current plan ends. You can't switch paid
  plans part-way through.

If you leave the free plan and pay offline, you have no active plan until the admin
approves the new one.

## Your plan history

Go to **Plans >> History** to see every plan you have had, with its **Status**
(**Active**, **Pending**, **Expired** or **Canceled**), start and end dates, payment
method and price.

<ImagePopup src="/images/choosing-a-plan/plan-history.png" alt="The Plan History page" />

## What your plan controls

| Plan detail | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Max Products** | How many products you can list. At the limit, creating a product or a bulk upload shows **You have reached max limit of … products.**, and the admin can't approve products past it. |
| **Max Staff Ids** | How many team members you can add. See [Users & Roles](../seller-panel/users-and-roles.md). |
| **Admin Commission** | The commission the store takes from your sales while the plan is active. |
| **Product Types** | The kinds of product you can create and sell. |
| **Permissions** | The menus you and your team can use. |

## When your plan ends

- You receive reminder emails 9, 7, 3 and 1 days before your plan ends, and on desktop
  screens the Seller Panel header shows **Subscription expires in:** with a countdown for
  the last nine days. Both stop once your next plan is active. Buy your next plan before
  then to keep selling.
- When a plan ends without another one to follow, you are back to **My Profile** and
  **Plans** only, and your products are hidden from customers.
- If a new plan allows fewer products than you have, the products over the limit are
  switched off and need the admin's approval again.

## Plan emails

| Email | When |
|---|---|
| **Subscription Created** | You buy a plan |
| **Subscription Activated** | Your plan becomes active |
| **Subscription Cancelled** | The admin cancels your plan, or you cancel a PayPal payment |
| **Subscription Expired** | Your plan ends |
| **Subscription expires in … day(s)** | 9, 7, 3 and 1 days before your plan ends |

No email is sent when a plan is cancelled because you bought another plan, or because
the admin deleted the plan.

For how the admin sets up plans, see
[Plans & Subscriptions](seller-subscription-management.md).
