# Seller Reporting

Reporting gives a seller a clear picture of how their store is doing — sales, commission, products, and customers — over a date range they choose, with trends charted over time.

::: info What you'll learn
- What reports a seller gets
- How to read and filter them
:::

## Sales

Open **Reporting → Sales** in the Seller Panel. Pick a **date range** and see headline figures such as **Total Sales** and **Total Commission Paid**, each with a **Sales Over Time** chart that compares the selected period against the previous one.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-reporting/reporting-sales.png" alt="Seller sales report" />

## Products and customers

Alongside sales, Reporting breaks performance down by:

- **Products** — how individual listings are selling, so you can spot your best and slowest movers.
- **Customers** — buyer activity for your store.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-reporting/reporting-products.png" alt="Seller product report" />

Each report respects the date-range picker and can be **exported** for offline analysis.

::: tip
Compare the same range month over month to see whether a change — a new listing, a [promotion](/marketing), or a price cut — actually moved your numbers.
:::
