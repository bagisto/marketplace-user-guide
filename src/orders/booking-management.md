# Booking Management

Sellers can list **bookable** products — items or services a customer reserves for a date or time, such as an appointment, rental, or event ticket. When a customer books one, the reservation appears in the seller's **Sales → Bookings**.

::: info What you'll learn
- What a bookable product is
- Where a seller sees their bookings
- How bookings relate to orders
:::

## Bookable products

A booking product is created like any other listing — choose **Bookable** as the product kind in the [create flow](/catalog/product-management#creating-a-product). Bookable products carry availability (dates, slots, or quantities) that customers pick from when they reserve.

## The bookings list

Open **Sales → Bookings** in the Seller Panel to see every reservation made against your bookable products, with the booking details and the order it belongs to.

<ImagePopup src="/images/booking-management/bookings-list.png" alt="Seller bookings list" />

A booking is placed through the normal checkout, so each reservation also appears in [Order Management](/orders/order-management) — you invoice, fulfil, and get paid for it the same way as any other order.

::: tip
Keep your bookable product's availability accurate so customers can only reserve slots you can actually honour.
:::
