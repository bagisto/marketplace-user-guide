# Featured Sellers

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--customer">Customer</span><span class="role role--admin">Admin</span></div>

Featured sellers give your best sellers extra visibility. The
**Our Featured Sellers** block appears on the marketplace landing page, between
the features and the seller journey. It isn't shown on the store's home page.

<ImagePopup src="/images/featured-sellers/featured-sellers-block.png" alt="The Our Featured Sellers block on the marketplace landing page" />

## What customers see

Each seller card shows:

- the seller's logo;
- their business name, which opens their shop page;
- their address;
- their star rating and number of reviews, counting approved reviews only.

## How sellers are chosen

The marketplace picks the sellers for you. You can't pin or hide a particular
seller.

A seller can be featured when they are:

- approved and not suspended;
- a shop owner with a business name, not a team member;
- selling on the current channel;
- on an active plan, when the store uses subscription plans.

The eligible sellers are then ranked by the **Popularity Criteria** you choose:

| Criteria | Ranks sellers by |
|---|---|
| **Maximum Orders** | Number of orders, whatever their status |
| **Maximum Products** | Number of products they list, including products waiting for approval and offers on other products |
| **Maximum Rating** | Average rating from approved reviews |
| **Maximum Sale** | Total value of their orders |
| **All** | Orders first, then products, rating and sales to break ties |

The top sellers, up to the **Limit Count**, are shown. When no seller qualifies, the
block isn't shown.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Configure >> Marketplace >> Featured Sellers**.
2. Switch **Status** on.
3. Set the **Limit Count** and **Popularity Criteria**.
4. Click **Save Configuration**.

The block also needs **Show Publicly** switched on in
[Sellers settings](../configuration/sellers.md). See
[Featured Sellers settings](../configuration/featured-sellers.md).
