# Seller Shop Page

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--customer">Customer</span><span class="role role--seller">Seller</span></div>

Every seller has a public shop page with their products, reviews and store policies.
It opens at `/marketplace/` followed by the seller's slug, for example
`/marketplace/urban-threads`.

Customers reach it from the **Sold By** link on a product, from the featured sellers,
or from a link the seller shares. Sellers open their own with **Visit Shop** in the
Seller Panel.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-shop-page/shop-page.png" alt="A seller's shop page" />

## The top of the page

- The seller's banner, logo, business name and address.
- Their star rating and number of reviews.
- **Share**, which lists the seller's social media links.
- **Contact**, to send the seller a message by email.
- **Report Issue**, for customers who bought from the seller, when the store lets
  customers report sellers. See
  [Seller & Product Flagging](../moderation/seller-product-flagging.md).

When the store has **Show Red Flag** switched on, a red flag next to the name means
customers have reported this seller more often than the store allows. It counts every
report the seller has received.

## Contact the seller

1. Click **Contact**.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, **Subject** and **Query**.
3. Click **Submit**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-shop-page/contact-seller.png" alt="The Contact form on a seller's shop page" />

You'll see **Your query has been submitted successfully**. The seller receives your
message by email and can reply to your email address. Anyone can use this form,
without signing in.

To keep a conversation with a seller in your account instead, use
[Communications](../customers/communications.md).

## Products

The **Products** tab lists the products the seller created, with how many there are.

- Use the filters on the left, including a **Price** range, to narrow the list.
- Use **Sort By** to order products from **A-Z**, **Z-A**, newest, oldest,
  cheapest or most expensive.
- Choose how many to **Show** per page, and switch between grid and list.
- Use **Search for Products** to search this seller's products.
- Click **Load More** to see more.

Catalog products the seller sells as their own don't appear here. Customers find those
offers on the product's own page. See [Shopping Across Sellers](shopping-across-sellers.md).

## Reviews

The **Reviews** tab shows the seller's average rating, how the ratings split across one
to five stars, and the five latest approved reviews. When there are five or more, click
**View All Reviews** to see the rest.

### Review a seller

Customers who have ordered from the seller can review them once.

1. Sign in to your customer account.
2. Open the seller's shop page and click the **Reviews** tab.
3. Click **Write a Review**.
4. Choose a star rating, and enter a **Title** and **Comment**.
5. Click **Submit**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-shop-page/write-review.png" alt="The Write a Review form for a seller" />

You'll see **Seller Reviewed successfully**. Your review appears once the store admin
approves it. See [Reviews & Ratings](../moderation/rating-management.md).

## About

The **About** tab shows the seller's business description, any other details the store
shares publicly, and their **Shipping Policy**, **Return Policy** and
**Privacy Policy** when the seller has written them.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-shop-page/about-tab.png" alt="The About tab with the seller's policies" />

Sellers edit all of this on [Manage Profile](../seller-panel/manage-profile.md).

## When a shop page isn't available

A shop page can't be opened when:

- the store doesn't make seller shops public (**Show Publicly** in
  [Sellers settings](../configuration/sellers.md));
- the seller isn't approved, or is suspended;
- the seller doesn't sell on the channel you're browsing;
- the store uses subscription plans and the seller has no active plan;
- the marketplace is switched off.
