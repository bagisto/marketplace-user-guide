# Marketplace Landing Page

The marketplace landing page is the pitch you make to people who might sell on your platform. It sits at **/marketplace** on your storefront, and every word and picture on it is set from the admin panel — no theming required.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where the landing page is configured
- What each section of the page holds
- How the page changes for someone who is already a seller
:::

## Where it's configured

Go to **Configure → Marketplace → Landing Page**. It has four sections, and they appear on the page in this order: **Banner**, **Highlights**, **Features**, the featured-seller showcase, and **Seller Journey**.

## Banner

The hero at the top of the page.

**Banner Title** and **Banner Description:** The headline and the sentence under it.
**Banner Button Title:** The caption on the call-to-action button.
**Banner Image:** The picture beside the text.

The button leads a visitor to the [seller registration form](/sellers/seller-registration). For a visitor who is **already signed in as a seller**, it becomes **Visit Shop** instead and points at their own store page — so the page never invites an existing seller to sign up again.

## Highlights

Four numbers that make the marketplace look established. Each is a bare value you type; the page supplies the caption:

**Community Count:** Shown as **Seller Community**, with a **+** appended.
**Business Hour:** Shown as **Online Business**.
**Payment Duration:** Shown as **Days\* Payment**.
**Serviceable Pincode:** Shown as **Serviceable Pincode**, with a **+** appended.

Because the captions are fixed and the **+** is added for you, enter only the figure — **500**, not "500+ sellers".

## Features

Why someone should sell with you.

**Feature Title** and **Feature Description:** The section heading and its introduction.
**Feature Image:** The image beside the four boxes.
**Feature Box 1–4:** Four selling points, each with an **Icon**, a **Title**, and a **Description**.

## Featured sellers

Between Features and Seller Journey the page shows **Our Featured Sellers** — real sellers, ranked and picked automatically. Nothing about it is set here; it comes from **Configure → Marketplace → Featured Sellers**, and it only appears when that showcase is switched on. See [Featured Sellers](/sellers/featured-sellers).

## Seller Journey

The last section walks a prospective seller through what joining involves.

**Journey Title** and **Journey Description:** The section heading and its introduction.
**Journey Step 1–5:** Five steps, each with an **Icon**, a **Title**, and a **Description**, numbered **Step 1** to **Step 5** on the page.

You don't have to fill all five — describe the journey your marketplace actually has.

::: tip
Write the Seller Journey as the answer to "what happens after I click that button?" — registration, approval, listing a first product, the first order, getting paid. A prospective seller weighing your marketplace against another is looking for exactly that, and it's the section most marketplaces leave at its defaults.
:::
