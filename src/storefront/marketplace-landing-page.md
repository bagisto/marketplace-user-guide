# Marketplace Landing Page

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--customer">Customer</span><span class="role role--admin">Admin</span></div>

The marketplace landing page invites visitors to open a shop on your marketplace. It
explains why sellers should join, shows your featured sellers, and links to the seller
sign-up form.

## Open the page

Click the store icon in the storefront header, next to the cart. The page opens at
`/marketplace` on your store's address.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketplace-landing-page/header-sell-icon.png" alt="The store icon in the storefront header" />

## What visitors see

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketplace-landing-page/landing-page.png" alt="The marketplace landing page" />

From top to bottom:

1. **The banner** — a heading, a short description, an image and a button that opens
   the **Become Seller** form. Below it, **Already have an account?** links to
   **Sign In**. A seller who is already signed in sees **Visit Shop** instead, which
   opens their Seller Panel.
2. **Highlights** — four figures: **Seller Community**, **Online Business**,
   **Days\* Payment** and **Serviceable Pincode**.
3. **Features** — a heading, a description, an image and four boxes explaining why
   sellers should join.
4. **Our Featured Sellers** — your most popular sellers. The block shows when featured
   sellers are switched on, seller shops are public, and at least one seller qualifies.
   See [Featured Sellers](featured-sellers.md).
5. **The seller journey** — five steps, from signing up to getting paid.

## Change the content

The headings, descriptions, images and figures on the page are set in
**Configure >> Marketplace >> Landing Page**. See
[Landing Page settings](../configuration/landing-page.md). A few labels are fixed and
can't be changed there: the four highlight labels, the **Our Featured Sellers** heading
and the line below it, and the **Step** labels.

Visitors who click the banner button follow the steps in
[Becoming a Seller](../sellers/becoming-a-seller.md).

## When the page isn't available

The page isn't available while the marketplace is switched off. See
[Turning the marketplace off](../configuration/overview.md#turning-the-marketplace-off).
