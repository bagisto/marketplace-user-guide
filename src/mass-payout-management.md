# Mass Payout Management

When a marketplace has many sellers with pending earnings, paying them one at a time is slow. **Mass payout** lets the admin settle multiple payable orders — across one or many sellers — in a single action, from the same Seller Payout Management screen.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where mass payout lives
- How to pay several sellers or orders at once
- What the payout summary tells you
:::

## The payout view

Open **Marketplace → Sales → Payment Requests** (**Seller Payout Management**). The summary cards give you the whole picture at a glance — **Payable Orders**, **Unpaid Payout Amount**, **Total Commission Earned**, and **Unpaid Sellers** — so you know how much is outstanding before you pay.

<ImagePopup src="/images/mass-payout-management/payout-management.png" alt="Seller Payout Management with mass pay" />

## Paying in bulk

Select the payable orders you want to settle using the row checkboxes, then use the bulk **Pay** action to pay them together. Each settlement records the amount paid and reduces the seller's remaining balance, exactly as an individual [payment](/payment-management) does — just processed as a batch. Payments are logged under **Marketplace → Sales → Transactions**.

::: tip
Use the summary cards and filters to target a payout run — for example, clear all fully-invoiced orders for the month in one pass rather than chasing sellers individually.
:::
