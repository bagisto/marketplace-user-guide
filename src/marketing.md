# Marketing

Sellers can run their own promotions from the **Marketing** section of the Seller Panel — automatic **Product Discounts** on their catalog, and **Checkout Deals** at the cart — and can also opt into promotions the admin publishes.

::: info What you'll learn
- How a seller creates a Product Discount (percentage or fixed)
- How a seller joins a Product Discount published by the admin
- How Checkout Deals apply promotions at the cart
:::

## Product Discounts

Open **Marketing → Product Discounts** and select **Create Product Discount**. The form is split across three tabs — **Discount**, **Details**, and **Advanced**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/product-discount-create.png" alt="Create Product Discount form" />

On the **Discount** tab, choose how the reduction is applied:

- **Percentage off the price** — take a percentage off every product the discount matches.
- **Fixed amount off the price** — take a set amount off each matching product.

Enter the **Discount Amount**, then use **Details** to name the discount and set its active dates, and **Advanced** to control which products it matches and its priority. Save it, and the reduction applies automatically to the seller's matching products on the storefront.

### Joining an admin Product Discount

The admin can publish discounts for sellers to opt into. From **Marketing → Product Discounts**, select **Join Product Discounts** to see what's available and enroll your products.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/join-product-discounts.png" alt="Join admin product discounts" />

Enroll (or withdraw) individually, or select several and use the bulk actions. Once enrolled, the admin's discount applies to your listings for as long as you stay joined.

## Checkout Deals

Open **Marketing → Checkout Deals** and select **Create** to build a cart-level promotion — the kind that rewards a whole order rather than a single product (for example, a discount once the cart passes a threshold), optionally gated behind a coupon code.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/checkout-deal-create.png" alt="Create Checkout Deal form" />

Set the deal's conditions and the reward it grants, and — if you want it applied only when a code is entered — enable and configure the coupon. The deal then applies to qualifying carts at checkout.

::: tip
Product Discounts change a product's shown price; Checkout Deals act on the cart total. For a simple scheduled price cut on individual products, the [Manage Pricing](/manage-pricing) sale price is often quicker.
:::
