# Seller Reports

<div class="page-roles"><span class="role role--seller">Seller</span></div>

Reports show how your shop is doing over any period, with each figure compared with the
period before. There are three reports: **Sales**, **Customers** and **Products**.

Go to **Reporting** in the Seller Panel and choose a report.

## Choose the period

Use the date picker at the top right. Pick a preset, such as **Today**,
**Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, **This Month** or **Last Year**, or choose your own
dates. Reports open on the last 30 days. If you sell on more than one channel, choose
the channel too.

Cards with a total, such as **Total Sales**, show the figure for the period, how much it
changed from the period before, in green or red, and a chart. Cards that list products
or customers show the top five as bars.

## Sales

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-reporting/reporting-sales.png" alt="The Sales report" />

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Total Sales** | Your sales over time |
| **Total Commission Paid** | The commission taken from your invoiced sales |
| **Purchase Funnel** | Products added to the cart, and purchases. Visitors and product views aren't counted, so they show 0. |
| **Abandoned Carts** | Carts left without ordering, their value, and the products left behind |
| **Total Orders** | Orders over time |
| **Average Order Value** | The average value of an order over time |
| **Tax Collected** | Tax over time, and the top tax categories |
| **Refunds** | Refunds over time |
| **Top Payment Methods** | The payment methods customers used most |

## Customers

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-reporting/reporting-customers.png" alt="The Customers report" />

- **Total Customers** — customers over time.
- **Customers With Most Sales**, **Customers With Most Orders** and
  **Customers With Most Reviews** — your best customers.
- **Top Customer Groups** — the customer groups that buy from you most.

Customer email addresses are masked when the store doesn't let sellers see customer
details.

## Products

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-reporting/reporting-products.png" alt="The Products report" />

- **Sold Products Quantity** — how many products you sold over time.
- **Products Added To Wishlist** — how many of your products were added to wishlists.
- **Top Selling Products By Revenue** and **Top Selling Products By Quantity**.
- **Products With Most Reviews**.

## See the details and export

1. Click **View Details** on a card.
2. Choose the start and end dates, and the channel. For most charts, also choose
   whether to group by **Day**, **Month** or **Year**. **Total Commission Paid** is
   always shown by day.
3. To download the table, click the export icon and choose **Export CSV** or
   **Export XLS**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-reporting/report-details.png" alt="The details of a report with the export menu" />

**Purchase Funnel** has no details page, so it can't be exported.

Team members need **Reporting**, with **Sales**, **Customers** and **Products** as needed.
