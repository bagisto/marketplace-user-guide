# Seller Reporting

Reporting gives a seller a clear, visual picture of how their store is doing — sales, commission, products, and customers — over a date range they choose, with every metric charted against the previous period.

::: info What you'll learn
- What each report covers
- How to read and filter the charts
- How to drill in and export
:::

## Sales

Open **Reporting → Sales**. Pick a **date range** (and channel, if you have more than one) and see headline figures — each as a card with its current value, the **percentage change** versus the previous period (green up, red down), and an **over-time chart** comparing the two periods.

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-reporting/reporting-sales.png" alt="Seller sales report" />

Sales metrics include **Total Sales**, **Total Commission Paid**, **Average Order Value**, **Total Orders**, a **purchase funnel**, **abandoned carts**, **refunds**, **tax collected**, and **top payment methods**. Each card's **View Details** opens a breakdown table you can **export**.

## Products and customers

Alongside sales, Reporting breaks performance down by product and by customer:

<ImagePopup src="/images/seller-reporting/reporting-products.png" alt="Seller product report" />

- **Products** — total sold quantities, wishlist adds, top sellers by **revenue** and by **quantity**, and the products with the most reviews.
- **Customers** — total customers, and your customers with the most **sales**, most **orders**, and most **reviews**, plus your top customer groups.

Every report respects the date-range picker and can be exported for offline analysis.

::: tip
Compare the same range month over month to see whether a change — a new listing, a [promotion](/marketing/product-discounts), or a price cut — actually moved your numbers.
:::
