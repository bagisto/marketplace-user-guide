# Payment Management

Payment Management is how sellers get paid for what they've sold. A seller requests payment for their fulfilled orders, and the admin settles it from a central **Seller Payout Management** view that tracks what's owed and what's been paid.

::: info What you'll learn
- How a seller requests payment for an order
- How the admin reviews and pays sellers
- What the payout view tracks
:::

## Requesting payment (seller)

After a seller fulfils an order, they can **request payment** for their earnings on it from the Seller Panel (**Sales → Orders → the order**). The request carries the seller's earning — the order total minus [commission](/commission-management) — and goes to the admin for settlement. The order's **seller payment status** then reflects where the request stands.

## Paying sellers (admin)

The admin manages every request under **Marketplace → Sales → Payment Requests** — the **Seller Payout Management** screen. Summary cards across the top show **Payable Orders**, **Unpaid Payout Amount**, **Total Commission Earned**, and **Unpaid Sellers**, and the grid lists each payable order with its gross amount, remaining total, seller total invoiced, and total paid.

<ImagePopup src="/images/payment-management/payout-management.png" alt="Admin Seller Payout Management" />

The admin pays a seller from here, which records the amount paid and updates the remaining balance. Every settlement is logged under **Marketplace → Sales → Transactions**, giving a full payment history per seller.

::: tip Settle many at once
To pay several sellers or orders together, use the bulk pay action on this screen — see [Mass Payout Management](/mass-payout-management).
:::
