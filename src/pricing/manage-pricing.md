# Manage Pricing

**Manage Pricing** lets a seller set the price of every product — and schedule a sale — from a single screen, without editing products one by one.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where to find Manage Pricing in the Seller Panel
- How to set a product's base price and a scheduled sale price
:::

## Opening Manage Pricing

In the **Seller Panel**, go to **Pricing**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/manage-pricing/manage-pricing.png" alt="Manage Pricing screen" />

Each row is one product with two price fields you can edit inline:

- **Price** — the product's regular selling price.
- **Sale Price** — an optional discounted price. Set the **Valid From** and **Valid To** dates to schedule when the sale runs; leave them empty for an always-on sale price.

Update the values and save the row. The new pricing applies to your listing on the storefront immediately (or when the sale window opens).

::: tip
The sale price only takes effect if it's lower than the regular price and inside its valid date range. For catalog-wide or cart promotions instead of a per-product sale, use the seller **Marketing** section (Product Discounts and Checkout Deals).
:::
